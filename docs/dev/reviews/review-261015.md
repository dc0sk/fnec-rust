# Reference-convergence audit — 2026-10-15 (main a9c1524)

A rule was added to the test-integrity skill (coding-agent-skills #47): a reference's own converged value must be **shown** converged. The capture runs the reference at more than one mesh multiplier and shows that the reference has settled within the gate's tolerance. A reference that drifts without settling is no reference there. FND-252 is the case that motivated it: at a radius step nec2c reads R 81.7 → 88.0 over 10 → 270 segments per arm.

This audit applies the rule to every nec2c figure the repository stores. Fable reviewed the design before any code ran, and three of its points changed the method:

- **The settle criterion.** "The two finest agree" is necessary, not sufficient, because a slow drift takes small steps. The audit measures the contraction ratio instead.
- **The standing gap.** `converged_reference.rs` asserts that the series contracts and nothing more, and the FND-252 series would pass it.
- **nec2dxs's role.** 4nec2's nec2dxs has the same NEC-2 lineage as nec2c, so it serves as a filter for port faults, not as a second reference.

The results are recorded in the ledger as FND-255 to FND-260.

## Method

1. **Inventory.** Four agents read the 69 files that quote nec2c and recorded every figure.
   - 574 figures in all: 404 back a gate and 170 are comment-only.
   - For each figure, the agent recorded the gate's kind, its tolerance and the mesh the capture was taken on.
   - It also rebuilt the deck from the test code and re-ran nec2c on it.
   - The rest of the repository's references were taken from their own files: the corpus (`corpus/reference-results.json`, 15 nec2c references) and the parity goldens (`apps/nec-cli/tests/parity_nec2c.txt`, 33 rows). The parity decks were dumped from `parity_sweep.rs` itself.
2. **Echo.**
   - 399 of the 404 gated figures reproduce at their stated capture mesh.
   - So do all 33 parity rows and every corpus figure. One corpus case needed the documented RP-field normalisation.
   - The mismatches are listed under FND-259.
3. **Series.**
   - nec2c ran at S = 1, 3, 9 and 27 through `scripts/nec2c-fine-mesh.py`, which puts every segment reference at the same point.
   - A multiplier the script refused (thin-wire limit, `GM`, plane-wave `EX`) was recorded as refused, not skipped.
   - Plane-wave decks ran through a local copy that passes `EX 1–3` through (a plane wave names no segment), with the thin-wire check intact.
   - Currents were read at each original segment's centre sub-segment.
4. **Settle criterion.** From the last three multipliers a < b < c, each a tripling:
   - r = |v(c) − v(b)| / |v(b) − v(a)|;
   - tail = |v(c) − v(b)| · r / (1 − r).
   - A reference is **settled** when r < 0.7 and tail ≤ tol / 3. The /3 leaves the subject two thirds of the gate.
   - With fewer than three multipliers inside the thin-wire limit, the figure is **unverifiable**.
5. **Beyond the thin-wire limit.** A stored capture taken where segment/radius is ≤ 8 without EK was re-run at the same multiplier with EK, and the two were compared.
6. **Second engine.** nec2dxs11k ran under Wine at 1× and at the top multiplier of every corpus and parity deck. The largest difference from nec2c was 0.05 Ω, so no port fault turned up.

**Settled.** Everything not named below settled, including:
- 10 of 15 corpus references;
- all RP gains (identical at 1/3/9× to 0.01 dB);
- the plane-wave currents with loose (≥ 2 %) tolerances;
- every absolute ohm gate whose tolerance is several times nec2c's mesh movement.

**Parity goldens.** These are a same-mesh *parity* claim by design, and the audit classifies them rather than failing them. nec2c at the deck's mesh sits 0.4–10.3 % from its finest run. The square loops are widest (10 %) and the tees next (5 %), all inside the gate's 25 % cap.

## FND-255 — `converged_reference.rs` passes a drifting series

`every_stored_series_contracts_to_its_reference` asserts two things:
- `dist(9×, 3×) < dist(3×, 1×)`;
- the stored figure is the 9× point.

It compares nothing with a tolerance. The FND-252 series contracts and would pass, and the corpus split-V (FND-256) does pass today.

**Fix:**
- Assert the tail estimate against each case's `ExternalR/X_absolute_ohm` / 3.
- Store the 27× point where the thin-wire limit allows it, or the EK twin where it does not.
- Sabotage-check the new assertion with the FND-252 series as the known failure.

**Out of scope.** The two `bend_corner_nec2c.rs` rows sit at an anti-resonance, \|Z\| ≈ 3600 Ω. The corpus `tl-two-dipoles-linked-nseg0` R trips only on r = 0.71 with a last step of 0.08 Ω, so it is not listed.

**Fix:** decide per figure. The options are:
- gate against an independently converged value (the MPIE where it is validated, or pymininec);
- widen the tolerance to carry the measured tail;
- or demote the figure to approach-only.

## FND-258 — the off-apex inverted-V "converges" between two antennas

`an_off_apex_inverted_v_converges_to_nec2c` feeds segment 5 of 21 and segment 10 of 41. Those centres sit at 0.214 and 0.232 of the arm, so the two meshes describe two different antennas. nec2c's own figures for them differ by 770 Ω in X (−j1727 against −j959). The assertion `e41 < e21` therefore compares errors on different problems. Each reference is also the same-mesh figure (FND-257).

**Fix:** a feed position that both meshes hold, as the U beside it does (segment 6 of 11 and 11 of 21 are both the centre), and a converged reference for it.

## FND-259 — figures that are not their deck's

- **`mpie_ground.rs:17/18/33`.** The figures were captured at 21 segments (41 for the vertical), but the test solves 40. At 40, nec2c gives 67.615+j53.058, 88.368+j69.108 and 89.836+j38.620.
- **`mpie.rs:1360`.** "Converged ~79.6+j46.6" is the corpus dipole, with half-length 5.282 m. The test's dipole has half-length c/4f = 5.2779 m.
- **`validate.rs:1618`** (comment). The FND-179 figure 1.295+j26.62 is the tag-2 feed. The test feeds tag 1, which gives 1.2787+j26.35.
- **`gpu_rp_exec.rs:156` and `solve_session.rs:1547`.** Quoted "CPU and nec2c" −3.12 / −0.26 dBi; nec2c prints −3.13 / −0.27.
- **`parser_warnings.rs:31` and `nec_parser/src/lib.rs:14`.** The feet-unit deck behind nec2c 84.8+j45.8 is recorded nowhere and could not be reconstructed.
- **`mpie_farfield.rs:14`.** 2.15 dBi is the ideal dipole's directivity. nec2c on the test's 40-segment deck gives 2.17 dBi, inside the 0.1 dB gate.
- **`mpie_solver_cli.rs:99`.** "nec2c ~71.5 Ω" is the converged R. On the test's 20-segment deck nec2c gives 67.215.

## FND-260 — captures below nec2c's thin-wire limit; the remesh script's gaps

**Captures below the limit.**
- 46 gated figures and the corpus `dipole-loaded` reference were captured at 9× or 27×, where segment/radius ≤ 8 and no EK is used.
  - The 27× captures are the `graph_nec2c` Y/T family, the `graph_receive_nec2c` loaded T, `interior_joins`, the plane-wave T and the transverse inverted-V.
  - In that regime nec2c's thin-wire kernel is outside its documented validity, and nothing recorded a check.
- Re-run with EK at the same multiplier, every one agrees within 0.12 % in \|Z\| and in max \|ΔI\|/peak (`dipole-loaded`: −882.44 against −882.45). So the numbers stand. The check had simply never been made.

**The remesh script refuses too much.**
- `scripts/nec2c-fine-mesh.py` refuses plane-wave `EX 1–3` and `GM`. Neither is a fundamental limit: a plane wave names no segment, and `GM` with a tag increment maps as `GX` does.
- These refusals are why the 27× receive captures were made with unchecked copies of the script.

**Fix:**
- Map `EX 1–3` and `GM`.
- Below the limit, let the script emit the EK twin, and have the capture assert the two agree, as FND-232 did by hand.

## Not judged

- **Comment-only figures (170)** have no gate. The inventory lists them for their echo only.
- **Figures in relative-to-other gates** (for example, GR against its written-out twin at 1e-9) are about fnec against fnec. There, the nec2c figure only sets a scale.
- **Three `GM` decks and one `GC` deck** could not be remeshed. Their figures echo at the deck's mesh.
- **Some decks were not run** because the inventory pointed their figures at a sibling deck file, for example `mpie_loop__sq11`/`sq21`. Their gates are covered through the sibling.
