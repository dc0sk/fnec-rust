# Targeted review — 2026-09-28 (changes since review-260828, main = b662854)

**Scope.** What changed between `25fb641` (the base of `review-260828.md`) and
`b662854`: 54 commits, 145 files. Three independent reviewers (Fable), one per
area:

- solver core (`crates/nec_solver`);
- frontends and distributed execution (CLI, GUI, `fnec_py`, worker/SSH);
- gates, CI and release tooling (`scripts/`, `.github/`, `docs/release-process.md`).

**Method.** Each reviewer had to reproduce every finding with a concrete deck or
command, against nec2c 1.3.1 or an identity, and then try once to refute it.
Suspicions they could not reproduce are listed separately. The maintainer's
session re-ran the three high findings on `b662854` and reproduced each. The
rest stand on the reviewers' reproductions, which are quoted in the findings
ledger (FND-171 to FND-183).

It is a targeted review, not a whole-project audit: it looked where things
changed. Findings 1 and 4 below are older than the window; the window's changes
exposed them.

## The two findings that change what fnec can be trusted with

**FND-171.** `matrix.rs` adds each image as `elem(seg, image)`, and `elem` is
`∫G · cos α`. For a wire slanted 45° its own image is perpendicular to it, so
`cos α = 0` and the image does nothing. A 45° dipole 10 m over perfect ground
reads 79.505 + j46.847 with or without the ground, to every digit. nec2c gives
78.29 + j42.40 over ground and 80.15 + j51.39 in free space. Slopers and
inverted-V arms are among the most common HF antennas. This is the same missing
physics FND-162 stage 4 records for perpendicular wires in free space, where it
is small; through the ground image it is full-size. `--solver mpie` answers
correctly (78.07 + j42.10).

**FND-172.** Two copies of "is this straight?" disagree.
`merge_collinear_wire_endpoints` merges only below 1e-6 rad, and
`ConductorPath::is_trivial` calls a path straight below 4.5e-5 rad. In between,
the path is classified reducible, is not merged, and lands on the plain
basis's pairwise junction row: the junction class FND-162 calls garbage. A
10 m wire at 63° split into two cards with 4-decimal coordinates (kink
2.7e-5 rad): fnec 0.32 − j930 Ω, nec2c 119.8 − j71.1. Exports rounded to 4 or 5
decimals land in this window.

## Unconfirmed

- Distributed JSON and `SWEEP_POINTS` emit `tag: 0, seg: 0`, where the schema
  defines the EX feedpoint (code trace only; needs a live SSH worker).
- A mixed-radius 90° L drifts from nec2c with refinement, but nec2c itself moves
  there (NEC-2's stepped-radius weakness), so there is no clean reference.
- The corner quadrature grades only toward section ends. A near-crossing,
  unjoined, non-parallel source near a section interior is not exercised.
- The mint workflow's CI-green query emits one verdict per page. Past 30
  check-runs it would refuse a good commit: it fails closed, never open.

## Checked and found sound

- **Solver:** L, U, off-apex V, split-V, 170° and 150° hairpins, 10:1 segment
  ratio at a bend, a thick L, LD and TL/NT on bent geometry, EX 4 == EX 0 on the
  L. Ground contact (FND-082): a grounded sloper and an inverted-L. Sweeps re-lay
  the sections per frequency. FND-164's relative Tikhonov is present.
- **Frontends:** `pre_solve_error` and `diagnose` reach the CLI main path, all GUI
  solve paths, all three `fnec_py` entry points and the worker. Hallén and MPIE
  routing is a single decision everywhere. Current-source pricing is identical
  on all frontends. The worker's line cap, panic containment and refusals behave
  as tested.
- **Gates:** all 8 CI jobs are required and strict. The tag ruleset is active.
  The mint workflow evaluated its CI gate before tagging 0.19.0. The build lock
  fails closed. `check-asset-platform.py` fails the real host-built binary. The
  test-catalog checker catches four kinds of sabotage.
